# CSST Website — Staff Guide

The CSST website updates itself when you send an email. No passwords, no dashboards, no code. Just email.

---

## The CMS Email Address

**Send updates to:** `updates@csrockets.org` *(or whatever address was set up — check with your IT contact)*

**Who can send:** Only authorized staff email addresses work. If your email isn't authorized, the system will ignore it. Contact your tech team to add your address.

**How fast does it update?** About 2 minutes after you send. You'll get a confirmation reply when it's done.

---

## What You Can Do

### 📢 Post an Announcement

Announcements appear as a banner at the top of every page. Great for short-term notices.

```
Subject: Announcement

School will be closed Monday, September 1st for Labor Day.
Expires September 2nd.
```

```
Subject: Announcement

Spring Open House is April 15th at 6pm! Come see the school.
Link: /apply
Link label: RSVP Now
Expires April 16th.
```

> **Tip:** Include an expiry date so the banner disappears automatically. Otherwise it stays until you remove it.

## Tips

- **Be specific.** The AI reads your email literally. "Add an event next Thursday" works, but "Add an info night on October 9th at 6pm" is better.
- **One email, multiple changes.** You can do several things in one email — e.g. post an announcement AND add an event.
- **You'll always get a reply.** If something was unclear, the system will email you back asking for clarification instead of guessing wrong.
- **Authorized senders only.** Emails from unknown addresses are silently ignored — this keeps the site safe.

## Common Questions

**Can I update the FAQ?** Not yet via email — FAQs are edited directly in the code. Ask your tech team.

**Can I add photos to the celebration/Instagram feed?** Photos come from your Instagram account (@csrockets_cos). Just post to Instagram and they appear on the site automatically via the Behold.so feed.

**Can I change the main page copy or design?** Not via email — those are design changes that require your tech team.

**Something went wrong — the site didn't update.** Check your confirmation reply for an error message. If you didn't get one, verify your email address is on the authorized senders list.
